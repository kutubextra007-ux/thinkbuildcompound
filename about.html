About

Hi, I’m KB.

Same questions. A little further along.

I’m a technology leader at Amazon, an entrepreneur, and a lifelong learner.

For a long time, I’ve wanted a place to write things down. Not polished advice, just real experiences and what I’m learning along the way.

In March 2026, I completed 20 years in my career. I spent that day quietly in Sedona, thinking about what the next 20 should look like. One of the things I wrote down was to share more openly, be more available to mentor, and stop leaving things for “someday.” (I wrote about it here.) This site is me starting on that.

I shared it publicly on 2nd October 2026, Gandhi Jayanti. Gandhi called his autobiography The Story of My Experiments with Truth. He wrote openly about his doubts, his mistakes and what he was still figuring out. I’m nowhere close to that, of course. But I’ve always believed you can learn as much from someone’s honest experiments as from their successes. That’s what I want this journal to be.

It’s a personal journal, not a pitch. No newsletter pop-ups, nothing to sell. Just stories, experiments and lessons, written as I go.

Why “think. build. compound.”

Think

Stories, observations and conversations that help me see things a little differently.

Build

Products, systems and habits I’m working on, experimenting with and learning from.

Compound

Career, investing, health and relationships, and the small, consistent actions that add up to outsized results over time.

Think deeply. Build things that matter. Let time do the rest.
